# Brief: labels-dev — data audit of LoL label and time alignment

Question: are LoL training rows aligned with the market the way the live trader will see it? Verify with data, not by reading code alone (pipeline-opus reads code; you measure).

Data: `data/lol/processed/datasets/{training,validation,split,audit,market_seconds,game_features}.parquet`, raw livestats windows/details `data/lol/raw/lolesports/{windows,details}/<esports_game_id>/...` (format in `src/lol/livestats_frames.py`, `04_fetch_lolesports.py`), Telonex books for the map's tokens (`src/shared/utils/telonex_book.py`, `src/backtest/telonex_local.py`, catalog `data/lol/processed/telonex/catalog.parquet`), links `data/lol/processed/lolesports_links/links.parquet`, live archives `data/trader/<match_id>/` for LoL maps the trader played (find them by game; `src/trader/archive_paths.py`, `archive_types.py`).

Do (sample ~150 whitelisted validation maps across June–September plus every map with pause_count > 0 or a spawn gap > 90 s; keep intermediates under `work/labels-dev/`):
1. Clock audit. For each sampled map: wall time of the frame that became second 0 (`state_ts_us` at second 0), the first Telonex book update time for the map's tokens, the first time the mid leaves the pregame prior by > 2 ¢, the first kill time from livestats. Report the distribution of (first-mid-move − second-0) and (first-kill − second-0). If the market starts moving before our second 0 on many maps, the anchor is late. Compare with Dota on 30 maps (Dota datasets under `data/processed/`, see `src/shared/constants/dataset.py`).
2. Label clock. Pick maps with pauses (audit `pause_seconds > 0`). For a row at second s, is `signal_market_p_radiant_300s` the mid at wall(s) + 300 s or at the wall time of game second s + 300? Recompute both from `market_seconds.parquet`/Telonex and show which one the dataset matches. Then the same for the history lags in `game_features.parquet`. State what the backtest (`src/backtest/signals.py`, `lol_inputs.py` `synthetic_cutoff_pauses`) and the live trader do during a pause.
3. Lag audit. Cross-correlate per map the 1 s series of Δ`radiant_nw_adv` (from dataset rows at their `second`) with Δmid at offsets −30..+60 s; report the per-map argmax offset distribution, per month. The dataset's join assumes the market sees the state at +11 s; if the argmax sits at +4 s (market ahead of our join) or at +25 s, say so with the histogram. (leadlag-sol does an event study; you do per-map cross-correlation; both are wanted.)
4. Row integrity: duplicate (match_id, second), non-monotone `state_ts_us`, seconds with no frame (interpolated? how), `market_p_radiant` NaN/0/1 rows, rows where `market_p_radiant` equals the label exactly (stale market), per month. `skipped_*` columns in audit per month.
5. Spawn-gap maps: list maps with `state_ts_us(second 0) − link start` > 90 s (or whatever the audit exposes), their count per month and their backtest PnL in `w540lv6` vs the rest.

Deliver: a verdict on alignment (anchor, label clock, lag) with distributions, the share of maps affected per month, PnL of affected vs unaffected maps, and fixes (e.g. anchor on first book move / GRID clock, pause-aware labels) with the experiment.

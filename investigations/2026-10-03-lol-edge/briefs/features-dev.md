# Brief: features-dev — do the LoL features carry the information the market trades on?

Question: Dota and LoL share one 77-column catalog designed for Dota (net worth, XP, deaths, top-1/top-3 shares, prior, mid; `DOTA_XP_FEATURE_COLUMNS` in `src/shared/utils/dota_features.py`, base `FEATURE_COLUMNS` in `src/shared/utils/gbm.py`). In LoL the market may move on things we do not encode (first dragon, herald/void grubs, tower plates, baron, level-6 spikes, objective timers) or on gold that we measure badly. Measure feature informativeness in LoL vs Dota, and check the LoL feature construction.

Data: `data/lol/processed/datasets/{training,validation,game_features}.parquet`, raw livestats `data/lol/raw/lolesports/{windows,details}/<esports_game_id>/` (per-participant `totalGold`, `level`, `xp`?, `creepScore`, kills/assists, dragons/barons/towers/inhibitors per team in `window` frames; items in `details` — see `src/lol/livestats_frames.py`, `networth.py`, `04_fetch_lolesports.py`), Dota datasets under `data/processed/` (`src/shared/constants/dataset.py`), published models `data/lol/models/research` and `data/new_model/research`.

Do:
1. Univariate informativeness at matched seconds (120, 240, 360, 480): for each of the 17 base features and the 5 most important history columns, Spearman correlation and a 1-feature L1 tree-stump MAE gain against the 300 s label, LoL vs Dota, validation split. Also the mutual information of the feature set vs the label at each second. Where is LoL weaker?
2. Net worth check: for 100 maps, compare the dataset's `radiant_nw`/`dire_nw` with the livestats `totalGold` sums at the same frames and with GRID `NetWorth` where `scripts/record_lol_dual_feed.py` / `compare_lol_grid_livestats.py` left recordings (`data/lol/scratch/`, search the repo for their output paths). Report the mean/max discrepancy and whether the reconstruction (`networth.py`) lags or leads the real gold (e.g. item purchases on back vs gold earned continuously).
3. XP from level (`LOL_LEVEL_XP`, `xp_source=level`): step size and staleness vs the true `xp` field if livestats exposes it; how often `radiant_xp_adv` is 0 exactly in minutes 1–8 (both teams same summed level) and whether the model's splits rely on it.
4. Missing LoL signals: from livestats windows build per-second team counters for dragons, heralds/grubs, towers, plates (if present), baron, first blood, and measure their incremental explanatory power over the current 77 columns for the 300 s label at seconds 300–480 (a quick ridge/GBM-free check: residual of the published model regressed on the new counters; report R² of residual and the sign). If `details` frames hold item events, check whether a "large purchase" flag explains residual moves.
5. Market-only baseline: how much of the label is explained by the mid's own last 30/60/300 s change in LoL vs Dota (momentum/mean-reversion). If the market-only baseline matches the model in LoL, the game features add little at 11 s.

Deliver: the informativeness table LoL vs Dota, the net worth / XP accuracy numbers, the incremental value of the missing signals (R² and a directional-markout estimate), and the ranked feature changes worth a retrain, each with the exact prepare/train change needed (file:line) and the expected effect. Scripts under `work/features-dev/`.

# Brief: window-dev — why does training LoL on the whole map hurt the buy window, and what is the principled fix

Facts: the owner finds it absurd that more data (whole map) gives a worse buy-window model than less data (≤540 s). The 2026-10-02 report measured it: full-map training costs 0.22 ¢ of directional markout on rows <480 (3-member ensembles), picks fewer trees (63/52/50 vs 90/113/130 at the buy-window L1 minimum), and the tree count is not the whole story. In Dota, full-map training did not hurt (`docs/experiments/dota-train-540.md`: 540 vs full = tie). LoL now trains ≤540 again (`LOL_TRAIN_END_SECOND`, `src/lol/constants.py`; `slice_model_rows` in `src/lol/06_train_model.py`). The same ensemble serves SELL decisions after 480 s until the map ends — trained on rows ≤540 only.

Data: `data/lol/processed/datasets/{training,validation,game_features,split}.parquet`, Dota `data/processed/datasets/` (see `src/shared/constants/dataset.py`, Dota rows are lagged 10 s and sampled how? read `src/prepare_dataset/prepare_dataset.py` and `src/train_model/train_model.py` `slice_train_rows`), model params `src/shared/utils/gbm.py` (objective, leaves, learning rate, `MAX_BOOST_ROUNDS`, `sub90`), published models.

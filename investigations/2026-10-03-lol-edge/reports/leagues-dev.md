# leagues-dev: train-on-all-leagues vs trade-on-17 — does the population mismatch explain the LoL edge collapse?
Status: FINAL

## Verdict

The league mix explains roughly **half** of the late-third collapse; the rest is a within-league quality flip concentrated in specific events, not a global decay.

- **Mix (~55% of the shortfall):** the June–August carry came from MSI (+$87/map), LEC regular season (+$73), EWC (+$30), NACL (+$68) — all ended or shrank by late August. The late slot filled with **EMEA Masters Summer BO1 group games (96 of 421 late traded maps, model edge ≈ −0.4¢, net ≈ $0/seed)** and Hitpoint Masters (~50 maps, ≈ $0). At early per-league per-map rates the late mix is worth ~$19/map vs ~$27/map early.
- **Within-league (~45%):** dominated by **LEC, which swung +$5,878 → −$2,629/seed (−$8.5k ≈ 51% of the −$16.6k total decline, on 4% of maps)** — almost entirely playoff-stage maps where the model's own directional markout went negative (−1.2¢ entry-conditioned), i.e. the model is *wrong*, not unlucky. Plus LCS (−1.5¢), Hitpoint (−1.8¢), EMEA-Summer (−0.4¢) went ≤0 while NACL/LPLOL/LES/EBL/CBLOL stayed +4–8.5¢ and LCK's backtest PnL actually *improved* (+$5.6k).
- **The train/trade mismatch is real but is NOT the mechanism.** 56% of ≤540s train rows are leagues we never trade (37% non-whitelist + 19% LPL/LCK-CL no-feed), EMEA train data is 5 months stale and a different edition, EBL has 2 train maps, LRN 0. Yet validation edge correlates ~0 with train-row density (ρ=−0.05) and ~−0.15 with staleness — LPLOL's 5.7k train rows produce the best late edge (+8.5¢). Consistent with the 2026-09-08 whitelist-retrain loss: league-conditional capacity is not the binding constraint; **which events we trade** is.
- **Actionable beyond the brief:** `World Star Challengers Invitational` — a tournament that did not exist when the whitelist was frozen (2026-09-08) — has 59 Sep validation maps, all with books+onchain fills, and the **best late-window model edge of any league (+8.7¢ entry markout, mae_gain +0.96¢)**. It is excluded by name. Whitelist admission is a manual, stale list; the calendar moves faster than the list.

## Findings

1. **Population mismatch quantified (verified).** Train = 2,713 maps / 994,477 labeled rows `second ≤ 540`. Shares of train rows: never-traded leagues 36.9%, whitelisted-but-no-live-feed (LPL 158k + LCK-CL 33k) 19.3%, effectively-traded leagues **43.9%**. Validation = 2,195 maps / 945,018 rows ≤540 (840,345 rows <480, 2,077 maps scored); backtest traded 1,239 of them. Command: `a_population.py` output T4.

2. **Late-window traded mix (verified).** 421 maps ≥2026-08-22: EMEA Masters 96 (22.8%), Hitpoint 53, LEC 50, LCK 48, LCS 40, CBLOL 30, RoL 25, LPLOL 22, NACL 19, EBL 18, LES 12, LRN 8. EMEA Masters train data is all Mar–Apr (Spring edition; 21 of 38 Sep teams absent from the train pool); Sep is the Summer edition — BO1 Swiss groups (50 maps) + early BO3 (46). `a_population.py` T5/T7, `d_lec_deep.py`.

3. **Model quality per league×month (verified).** Published ensemble `20261003T091511Z` (= histfix model, identical member_trees/metrics) scored on all validation rows `second<480` via the same `load_lol_dataset`+`attach_catalog_features` path as training (dense tape, GRID_HISTORY_POLICY). Entry-conditioned directional markout `dir_entry` (|Δ|≥0.02, price 0.45–0.85), whitelisted, late window:
   - **≤0¢:** LEC −1.22, LCS −1.46, EMEA Masters −0.39, Hitpoint −1.76, LCK-CL −0.39 (untraded)
   - **>0¢:** LCK +0.92, RoL +1.82, EBL +3.96, LES +4.21, CBLOL +4.56, NACL +5.59, LPLOL +8.53, **LPL +5.82 (untraded)**, **WSCI +8.7 (unwhitelisted)**
   Whitelisted pooled by month: mae_gain 0.33→0.25→0.22→0.16¢; dir_entry 2.9→2.2→2.6→2.0¢ — a steady decline, *then* the mix rotates into the flat cells in Sep. Sanity check: pooled all-league mae_gain reproduces model.json's 0.229¢ (row count identical, 840,345). Caveat: scored on the *dense* tape like the trainer — hist-dev covers received-tape parity. `b_score.py` + `scored_val_rows.parquet`.

4. **Backtest PnL per league (verified).** histfix-20261003r2, net = engine_pnl+rebate−taker, 3 seeds. Early (<Aug22) +$21.9k/seed over ~800 completed maps; late +$5.3k/seed over ~418. Late by league: **LEC −$2,629** (50 maps; worst 5 = −$3,326), Hitpoint −$149, LRN −$159, EBL −$110, EMEA −$35 | LCK +$5,565, LES +$1,222, LCS +$1,126, CBLOL +$163, NACL +$107, LPLOL +$98, RoL +$95. The five ≤0-edge league cells contain ~42% of late entry rows (17.5k of 41.4k, traded only) and their conditioned-markout mass is −27% of the late total; backtest net inside them −$1.7k/seed. `c_pnl.py`, `pnl_rows.parquet`.

5. **LEC is the single largest driver (verified).** −$8.5k/seed swing vs early. Losses cluster in 7 playoff-stage maps (fnc-gx 08-30, kc-mkoi 09-19, vit-mkoi 09-12, gx-navi 09-11, navi-fnc 08-24): positions of 2.9–4.3k shares bought at 0.52–0.75 and settled/expired near worthless; buy-fills markout_300s already −10 to −18¢ at entry (mid kept moving against the position after the fill). Split: BO3-g1-2 −$1.5k, BO3-g3+BO5 −$1.1k/seed. Not a feed or code issue — the model's LEC playoff predictions were directionally wrong on entry-size positions. `d_lec_deep.py`, fills/results inspection.

6. **The declining slice is BO1 + BO3-game-1/2, not "playoffs" (verified).** dir_entry early→late by format: BO1 +0.91→**−2.54**; BO3 g1-2 +2.85→**+0.63**; BO3 g3 +4.06→+4.68; BO5 g1-2 +0.60→+2.15; BO5 g3 +3.17→+2.32; BO5 g4-5 +2.98→+2.57. The most-watched games (BO5 playoff brackets) *kept* edge — market-efficiency-by-attention does not fit. What fits: the slice that died is **early-series games of end-of-season minor tournaments** (EMEA Summer Swiss BO1, Hitpoint, CBLOL/LEC/LCS BO3 openers), where the mid's link to game state weakened — corr(mid−prior, gold adv): EMEA 0.79→0.65, CBLOL 0.81→0.66, LCS 0.85→0.74, LEC 0.88→0.76 (overall 0.77→0.70). `b/c` joins, capture table.

7. **Patch correlation is a calendar confound (likely).** Traded maps by patch: 16.16 (Aug22-31, mostly LCK+LES) +$7.4k/seed mean; 16.17 (Sep playoffs, LEC36/Hitpoint43/LCS32) **−$1.1k/seed** (LEC −$2,454 inside it); 16.18 (Sep21-29, 96/99 maps = EMEA Summer) −$35/seed, dir −0.39¢. Edge survives *within* the same patches elsewhere (NACL/LPLOL/EBL +4–8.5¢ on 16.17), so the game-patch channel is not the cause; tournaments hosted inside each patch window are. Patch per match from `games.parquet.patch_version`.

8. **No measurable market/covariate shift at the aggregate level (verified).** Whitelisted, seconds<480: label |300s move| q50 = 7–8¢ and q90 = 20–22¢ every month; realized mid path vol ~8.4–8.7¢ flat; |prior−0.5| mean 0.15–0.19; feature dists (nw_adv, kills) stable. Order-event spread: majors ~0.01–0.02, minors 0.03–0.05 (EBL 0.05, Hitpoint 0.04, RoL 0.03) — Sep mix pulls the average up (0.0205 Jul → 0.0254 Sep). Minor leagues' own label vol *rose* in Sep (EBL 9.7→11.9¢, NACL 9.5→11.2, LES 7.9→9.3) — edge survived there anyway. `e_shift.py`, `marketvol_rows.parquet`, `order_events_s0.parquet`.

9. **Minor-league feed quality is worse — but not the mechanism (verified).** Median skipped-frame fraction (prepare invariants): Hitpoint 8.1%, EBL 10.0%, LPLOL 6.4%, NLC 6.0%, RoL 4.7% vs majors ~2.9%; usable rows/map: Hitpoint 794, EBL 652 vs ~1,700–1,900. Yet minor leagues are where the model still earns — feed quality does not rank-order edge either. `audit.parquet` join.

10. **Reweighting what-if (verified as argument, not proof).** Late-window dir_entry vs league train density ρ=−0.05, vs staleness ρ=−0.15; Jun–Jul dir_entry vs late ρ=+0.14 — **early league quality does not predict the late flips** (LEC 2.4→−1.2, LCS 6.7→−1.5). A league-density or static league-quality gate therefore buys ~nothing; a re-weight/retrain would only help if it changes the map inside the *specific* Sep events, which is untestable without a retrain. `f_reweight.py`.

11. **WSCI gap (verified locally; live-feed status unverified).** All 59 WSCI Sep maps have books + onchain fills + `eligible` in `backtest_audit.parquet`; scored edge is the best late (+8.7¢/entry, +0.96¢ mae_gain). Excluded only because `lol_league_whitelist.json` predates it. Live admission additionally needs `eventMetadata.gridSeriesId` — not checkable from local archives (gamma page files don't index it; the live metadata dir is VPS-side). Same class of check should run for `LPL` (big edge, no feed → dead anyway) and `Rift Legends`/`Circuito Desafiante`/`Prime League`/`LCP`/`LFL`/`TCL` (excluded leagues that were net-negative on the 2026-09-08 measurement — recheck on the Aug–Sep window before re-admitting).

12. **Production-model nuance (verified).** `production_training.parquet` covers all 2,195 validation maps including all 99 EMEA-Sep — the deployed model trains through Sep 29. The research model's staleness on EMEA-Summer is a *backtest* artifact; live trading had whatever production model was current at each date. The live problem is admission of edgeless events, not stale training rows.

## What I ruled out

- **"Filter the training set to the whitelist"** — already falsified (2026-09-08 experiment: −$1,039/seed, worse tails); my density-vs-edge ρ≈0 explains *why* it can't help: per-league edge does not come from per-league row count.
- **Global market-vol / feature-distribution shift** — label vol, realized mid vol, priors, nw_adv/kills all flat Jun→Sep (Finding 8).
- **"Playoff markets are efficient"** — BO5 playoff maps kept +2.3–2.6¢ model markout late; the dead slice is BO1/BO3-early (Finding 6).
- **Game-patch effect** — same patches keep edge in other leagues; the patch window is just the tournament calendar (Finding 7).
- **Feed/skip quality ranking edge** — minor leagues are both worst-fed and best-edged (Finding 9).
- **Team-pool churn as the LEC/major driver** — LEC/LCS Sep teams are 100% inside the train pool; EMEA has 21/38 new teams but that's the edition change, not a model-visible covariate (no team features exist anyway).

## Proposed experiments

Ordered by information-per-cost. All commands run from `esports-trader/` root; none are in my allowed set — the orchestrator runs them.

1. **Rolling admission-kill rule, replayed offline (no retrain, no code).** Hypothesis: a league/event gate that *reacts within days* beats any static filter. Exact test: over each backtest map's `results.parquet` + my `scored_val_rows.parquet`, simulate "stop opening positions in league L once its trailing 30-map entry-markout < 0" and count avoided late-window loss. Expected: removes most of the −$1.9k/seed bad-slice drag while keeping the early profits the same slices produced. Watch: late-third net + worst-map. Missing code: an admission hook keyed on trailing per-league markout (live: `lol_league_filter.py`/`match_worker.py`; backtest: league gate in `select_backtest_event_ids` is league-static today — needs a dynamic variant). Cost: offline sim ~1h work; live wiring is a real change.
2. **Walk-forward staleness test (small code + one retrain).** Train `train_research` on `production_training.parquet` rows restricted to `start_time < 2026-08-22` (covers Jul–Aug: MSI tail, EWC, LEC/LCS/LCK summer) and score Sep-only rows. Missing code: `main()` hardcodes `LOL_TRAINING_PATH` — add a `--train-path`/`--train-cut-ts` flag (3-line change; `slice_model_rows` already takes the frame). Expected if staleness is real: Sep dir_entry in EMEA/Hitpoint cells recovers ≥1¢. If it doesn't (my prediction, given ρ_recency=−0.15 and that the prod model already saw Sep data), the admission-side experiments are confirmed as the right lever. Watch: dir_entry by league on Sep.
3. **Recency-weighted ensemble (same test, one line more).** `lgb.Dataset(train_X, train_y, weight=exp(-age_days/τ))` in `fit_research_members` (τ≈60d). Same read-out as #2; answers "recency weighting" directly instead of by proxy. Cost: one retrain each.
4. **League/tournament covariate (needs prepare+train rebuild — expensive, medium priority).** Add `league` (or a coarse tier: major/regional/international) as a categorical feature in `LOL_DATASET_COLUMNS`/`DOTA_XP_FEATURE_COLUMNS` for LoL only. Expected: lets trees express "gold→mid map differs by tournament class"; likely small positive on the dead cells, risk of overfitting edition-level noise. Watch: mae_gain on late-third + per-league capture. Cheaper precursor: score whether the model's *residual* (model_p − future) is league-predictable — I can compute this on `scored_val_rows.parquet` in minutes on request.
5. **Whitelist refresh pass (config-level, cheap):** add WSCI pending a `gridSeriesId` check on the collector (`metadata/events/<id>.json`, VPS side or next archive sync); re-run the excluded-league PnL measurement of `lol-league-whitelist.md` §trade-time-filter on the *Aug 22–Sep 29* window only (the 17 leagues were selected on Jun–Aug data — selection is in-sample and stale). Expected: WSCI +59 maps of the strongest late edge; rechecks LFL/LCP/Rift Legends/Circuito/Prime League on a fresher window. Watch: late-third PnL with WSCI included.
6. **Per-league entry threshold — do not run.** Jun–Jul edge does not predict late edge (ρ=0.14); a static per-league threshold tuned on the backtest is in-sample and would have kept LEC on. Only revisit inside a rolling scheme like #1.

## Scripts

All under `work/leagues-dev/`; run from `esports-trader/` with `PYTHONPATH=src:scripts uv run python <script>` (nice -10). Artifacts: `split_with_league.parquet` (split+league+month+flags), `traded_match_ids.parquet`, `scored_val_rows.parquet` (840k scored rows: match/second/cur/future/model_p/delta/markout/entry/league/month), `pnl_rows.parquet` (per map×seed net/engine/rebate/buy300s + league/month/late), `marketvol_rows.parquet`, `order_events_s0.parquet`, `spread_league_month.csv`.

- `a_population.py` — T1–T7: maps & ≤540 rows per league×month for train/val/traded; train-row shares; late mix; train coverage of late leagues.
- `b_score.py` — loads `data/lol/models/research` (10 members), attaches GRID-tape features via `attach_catalog_features`, predicts, writes `scored_val_rows.parquet`, prints league×month quality tables.
- `c_pnl.py` — joins 3-seed `results.parquet`+`fills.parquet` (net = engine+rebate−taker) to leagues; per league×month PnL, early/late decomposition, mix counterfactual; writes `pnl_rows.parquet`.
- `d_lec_deep.py` — event slugs per league, LEC late per-map losses by event, team-pool overlap train vs Sep.
- `e_shift.py` — feature/label quantiles per month, per-league |move| medians, realized 300s mid vol per map, quote-events spread by league×month.
- `f_reweight.py` — train density/recency vs late edge; negative-edge cell mass; production-training coverage.
- `inspect_schemas.py` — parquet schema dump used at the start.

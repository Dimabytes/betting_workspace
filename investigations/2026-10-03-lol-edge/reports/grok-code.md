# grok-code: LoL signal path, training vs backtest vs live
Status: FINAL
## Verdict
LoL train, grid-v1 backtest, archive backtest, and live do not build the same 77-vector. Training (`attach_catalog_features`) pivots history at the exact lag second on the dense livestats tape, with `start_second=60`. Backtest and live pivot with `GRID_HISTORY_POLICY` (±30 s, drop the tick), and the grid-v1 tape has no seconds before 76, so a lag target of 60 is filled from the first feed tick (~16 s later) while training used second 60. On the earliest 20 validation maps that substitution is not a warmup footnote: `game_total_5m_*` is NaN through the first backtest tick (76) and first becomes finite at second 360 on 17/20 maps (the other three at their first labeled second ≥360). The constant 11 s is a market-clock shift in train and grid-v1; the live reducer never subtracts it. It subtracts the socket frame's `delay` from the GRID scoreboard. Archive replay then serves that live vector; grid-v1 serves the livestats vector. Same column names, two generators.

## What I ruled out
- LoL does not have its own `HistoryPolicy`. The running process shows LoL primary, Dota GRID primary, and `history_policy_for_columns(DOTA_XP_FEATURE_COLUMNS)` are one object (`start_second=60`, gap `30.0`, `drop_gap_ticks=True`). Oddin is the other object.
- The 11 s lag is not applied twice to the grid-v1 feature second. `lag_seconds=0` and the +11 s is only `decision_lag_ns` on `state_ts_us`.
- `game_total_5m_*` is not available at the first backtest tick. 0 of 20 maps have it finite at second ≤76 under the training attach. It is also not missing at 360 because the tape lacks second 60: all 20 tapes contain `game_second=60`. The three later maps (361, 364, 453) are missing labeled rows, not pivots.
- Top-3 ratio is not a LoL train-vs-live skew. Both sides call `build_top_player_features_over_total`. The skew vs Dota is real and is a different denominator, fit separately.
- Buy/sell thresholds are not a LoL fork. `BUY_CUTOFF_SECOND`, `MIN_ABS_DELTA`, and `EXIT_ABS_DELTA` are the shared strategy constants, and both games import `Follow300Policy`.

## Proposed experiments
The number to watch on every one of these is the late third (LoL validation maps from about 2026-08-22), buy-300 s markout and the share of decision rows with `|delta|>=0.02`. Totals will keep the June–August PnL.

1. **Pivot substitution, no fit.** On ~200 late-third whitelist maps, at each grid-v1 decision second, build the 60 history columns twice: `attach_catalog_features` gap 0 on `game_features.parquet`, and `SnapshotHistory(GRID_HISTORY_POLICY)` on the cadence tape that `build_match_signals` actually records (seconds ≥76). Report the fraction of decision rows where any column differs, and where `game_total_5m_radiant_nw_adv` differs, split at seconds 360–375 vs after 376. Then `load_predictor` on `data/lol/models/research` (the LIVE catalog) and score both matrices. Expect the 360–375 band to move `total_5m` by the net-worth change from second 60 to the first tick; the previous dense-vs-received delta shift was about 0.10 ¢, and this is the mechanism that would produce it on the late third. Cost: one `PYTHONPATH=src` process, a few hundred thousand rows, minutes, under 4 GB if match ids are filtered in pyarrow. No training.
2. **Same comparison on archive ticks.** For matches `resolve_lol_feed_plans` binds to a schedule, compare `replay_tape_history` on the archived ticks with gap 30 against gap-0 attach at `tick.game_second`. This is the live path. If the archive tape's first second is also past 60, the same 16 s class of snap is in production, not only in synthetic cadence.
3. **What `delay` actually is.** Scan `delay` on `series_table` frames in a sample of LoL `data/trader/<match_id>/grid_state.jsonl` (read-only, a few dozen maps). Compare the median to 11. If it sits near 0 or 8, live `second` is shifted relative to the training join by about `11 - delay` seconds and the model cannot see it. Cost: small JSONL read, no parquet rebuild.
4. **Confirming backtest, orchestrator-run.** Two arms, same seeds, late-third report only. Arm A: grid-v1 history built with gap 0 on the dense tape (serve what was trained), decisions and +11 s market clock unchanged. Arm B: leave serve logic, refit with `SnapshotHistory(GRID_HISTORY_POLICY)` on a tape that starts at 76 and drops gap ticks, so the fit sees the snap and the dropped rows. Arm A says whether today's backtest is punished by the pivot rule. Arm B is the one that can change live. Do not read the winner off total PnL.

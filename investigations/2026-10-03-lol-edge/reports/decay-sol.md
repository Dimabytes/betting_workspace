# decay-sol: LoL edge loss is concentrated within tradeable leagues and filled orders
Status: FINAL
## Verdict (≤10 lines)

1. **Verified:** at equal map count, early→late net PnL loses $9,176: $7,070 from lower engine ¢/share, $1,907 from lower purchased-share volume, $199 from rebate.
2. **Verified descriptive attribution:** league activity/mix explains $2,845 (31.7%) of the $8,977 engine decline; within-league PnL/map changes explain $6,132 (68.3%).
3. **Verified:** mix does not explain the per-share edge loss: realized engine-rate mix effect is −0.026¢, versus +1.982¢ within-league deterioration; LEC and LCS are the main gross deteriorators, with LCK offsetting them.
4. **Verified:** frozen-model entry DIR on backtest maps falls 2.272→1.394¢, while all-league DIR rises 1.926→2.616¢. Universal model staleness is an insufficient explanation.
5. **Verified:** filled-order drift after submission flips +1.746→−0.395¢; maker price concession improves +0.813→+0.918¢. Lost spread capture is not the culprit.
6. **Likely:** league-specific signal/calibration change plus worse selection of passive fills dominates; whether its cause is stale training, faster competition or feed timing remains unidentified.
7. There is no clean permanent Aug 22 step. Weekly performance is jagged, with an Aug 31 rebound. Prioritize time-held-out, league-stratified retraining and a paired quote-versus-fill selection test.

### 8. Ranked attribution and limits (verified arithmetic; causal ranking likely)

1. **Within-traded-league conditional return deterioration, especially LEC/LCS, with weaker subsequent movement on filled quotes.** $6,132 of engine decline under map-count standardization; +1.982¢ realized-rate loss under share standardization; +3.696¢ fill-markout loss before beneficial mix offsets. This is the strongest operational target. The conditional model relationship itself weakens on traded maps, but its event-cluster CI is wide. **Causal split between stale model, faster market and adverse selection: unmeasured.**
2. **Lower economic throughput and disappearance of profitable tournaments.** The quantity identity gives $1,907 net-loss contribution from lower volume, while league-count standardization gives $2,845 engine-loss contribution from mix; these describe the same loss differently and are **not additive**. MSI/EWC/KeSPA gross disappeared $6,083. Mix has almost zero adverse effect on realized ¢/share (−0.026¢ improvement), so it does not explain the underlying efficiency loss.
3. **Entry regime/second shifts.** Only +0.119¢ from elapsed-second mix and +0.086¢ from delta-bin mix; price mix improves by 0.228¢. There is no identified dollar treatment effect for changing these knobs.
4. **Broad feed/data failure or universal model age.** No numerical causal allocation is defensible; direct controls do not support them as sufficient explanations. A genuine league-specific market efficiency/feed-lag change remains plausible and needs the paired tests below.

## What I ruled out

- **A universal September collapse in model quality:** all-league LoL metrics hold up and excluded-map entry DIR improves; Dota entry DIR improves on the same dates.
- **Spread compression as the main loss:** recorded spread and captured submit-mid concession widen/improve rather than shrink.
- **Simply smaller deltas or more extreme entries:** forecast magnitude and average token price barely change, and reweighting them accounts for little or negative markout loss.
- **Moving entries into worse minute bins as the main explanation:** 94% of markout loss remains within the same elapsed-second bins.
- **The arrival of archived-schedule replay alone:** late synthetic maps have weak/negative offline signal and worse filled returns too.
- **An obvious broad pause/stamp break:** audit rates and clock residual medians are similar. This does not rule out league-specific or seconds-scale latency.
- **Statistically certain stale-model causality:** the backtest-population entry-DIR decline has a wide event-cluster CI crossing zero; all-league behavior contradicts a universal-age explanation.

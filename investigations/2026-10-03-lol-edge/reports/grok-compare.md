# grok-compare: Dota vs LoL design differences and ranked hypotheses
Status: FINAL
## Verdict
LoL really does have more maps and more backtest turnover than Dota, and less profit per map and per dollar. On the two LIVE catalogs (3 cadence seeds, $300 clip), LoL completes 1,221 maps and turns $875k for $27.2k net; Dota completes 809 maps and turns $508k for $36.8k net. Per completed map that is $22 vs $45; per dollar of buy notional, 3.1¢ vs 7.2¢. The gap is not in June–July markout (LoL July buy-300s 2.42¢, Dota 2.10¢). It opens in August and is large in September (1.15¢ vs 5.45¢), while LoL turnover stays higher. History columns and the ≤540 window do not touch that, because they do not change who has already priced the game.
The best account is the one in context fact 2, sharpened by the pipeline diff: we join LoL state 11s after the livestats frame, and the repo’s own probe says the mid moves at 4–5s, while a GRID widget sits on the Polymarket page ~50s ahead of the official stream. Dota’s kept execution lag is 10s against broadcasts that run ~27–30s behind a fast feed, so the same maker still has a lead. A static data bug is a weak primary cause: July already matches, and Dota trains on STRATZ and serves GRID (a bigger source switch) and still earns in September. Real train/serve skews exist (the `second` feature, net-worth reconstruction, spawn vs GRID clock) and are worth a cheap check; they are not sufficient to explain a gap that appears only in the second half of validation.

4. **The shared backtest clock assumes a Dota market-second column.** verified as a code difference; PnL effect speculative. Dota validation `second` is the market second, and the model is shown `second - 10`, which is the state second (`prepare_dataset.py:192-204`, `signals.py:543`). LoL stores the game second (`05_prepare_dataset.py:244`) and training feeds that second to LightGBM (`06_train_model.py:81-87`, `130-143`). The backtest still subtracts lag 11 from it (`signals.py:383`, `521`, `543`) and adds 11s to the wall clock before reading the book (`signals.py:381`, `405-414`). The book read matches training (the stored mid was already +11s, and LoL replaces it with a fresh lookup at the same delay). The `second` feature and the first-tick cut do not: first kept game second is 87, while `GRID_V1_FIRST_TICK_SECOND["lol"]` is 76. History levels can still land on the right game second, because the tape key is shifted by the same 11s as the pivot. grok-code is tracing this. It cannot be the whole Sep-vs-Jun gap, because the same code ran in June, when LoL buy-300s was 3.5¢.

5. **History and the 540 window were the wrong knobs for a lead/regime problem.** likely, from docs plus this table. LoL histfix vs the previous 77-col model was −$985 engine per seed, t p=0.68 (`docs/experiments/hist-policy-20261003.md:58-74`). Dota’s same history rule beat its 77-col model on every seed and was still not promoted; Dota LIVE remains `20261001T220726Z` with the old pivot rule (`hist-policy-20261003.md:8-10`, `68-70`). Dota ≤540 vs whole-map was a tie (paired p=0.57 on two seeds, `dota-train-540.md:61-62`; extra seeds p=0.69, `hist-policy-20261003.md:85-87`). Two-thirds of Dota’s training rows are past second 540 (finding 2) and that is the model earning 5.5¢ in September. LoL already measured full-map training as −0.22¢ DIR (context fact 5) and went back to ≤540. Adding 60 history columns on a state the market has already priced does not create a lead.

## What I ruled out
- **“LoL has more volume, so the model should make more money.”** The backtest already gives LoL 1.7× the buy notional and 0.74× the net. Volume is not the missing piece.
- **The $300 clip as the cause of the backtest gap.** Both LIVE manifests set `layer_usdc` 300, and median filled clips are $188 vs $175. Live clips do differ (LoL flat $5, Dota tiered to $400) and that matters for live dollars, not for this catalog comparison.
- **Buy/sell rules, horizon, ensemble recipe.** Cutoff 480, entry 0.02, exit level 0.015, L1, K=10 `default_sub90`, 77 columns are shared. Tree counts differ (mean 47 vs 69) because the row grids differ, not because the hyperparameters differ.
- **Minute-stride training as a new idea.** `docs/experiments/lol-minute-train.md` (2026-08-28) trained on `second % 60 == 0`, kept the 1Hz holdout, and DIR moved 0.560¢ → 0.516¢ with overlapping CIs. No backtest. A repeat is only justified if the late third and the 77-col catalog are the point; the old offline result did not support it.
- **Whitelist-only retrain, other horizons, exit model, cut 900, gold-velocity off.** All lost or were not promoted (`docs/experiments/README.md`: `lol-league-whitelist.md`, `lol-horizon-train.md`, `lol-exit-model.md`, `lol-cut900.md`, `lol-nwoff.md`). The whitelist gate stays; the filtered model does not.
- **“Train/live feature source mismatch” as something LoL has and Dota does not.** Dota trains minute rows on STRATZ XP-at-second and serves exact-second level XP plus live GRID (`stratz_seconds.py:218` vs `286`, `grid_feed.py:208`). That model’s September buy-300s is 5.45¢. LoL’s XP definition is the level table on all three paths. The LoL-only skew is net worth (reconstructed totalGold vs GRID `NetWorth`) and the clock in finding 4.
- **On-disk Dota validation month counts.** Hash mismatch, finding 3. A prepare is in progress; those rows were not used.
- **Book depth and named liquidity.** Not measured. Median clip says both games fill about 60% of the $300 rung. decay-dev / market-dev own the book.

## Proposed experiments
Judge every one on the late third (LoL 2026-08-22..09-29, ~406 maps): buy-300s, net, CVaR5, worst map. The catalog total is the June–August book.

1. **Kill or keep the 11s lead before any retrain.** Prior on this being the main gap: 0.45. Agent: leadlag-sol (tape), leadlag-dev (who else has GRID). On late-third maps versus June maps, at each death, measure seconds from the livestats frame wall to the first mid move >1¢, using `market_seconds.parquet` columns `match_id, second, state_ts_us` plus the mid. Confirm if the late-third median delay is still under 11s and shorter than June’s. Kill if the late-third median is past 11s. Cost: one read-only pass, no prepare. Only if it confirms, change `LOL_SOURCE_LAG_SECONDS` to the measured delay, re-prepare labels (not livestats), retrain, and run one 3-seed `lol-backtest` named apart from LIVE. Expected if the hypothesis is right: late buy-300s from ~0.5¢ toward the June 3¢. Cost of that second step: a LoL prepare plus a 3-seed backtest. Do not run it beside the Dota prepare.

2. **Rescore the `second` skew with no new fit.** Prior that it moves September: 0.15. Agent: grok-code for the trace; then one seed. In `build_match_signals`, for `game == "lol"` only, stop subtracting `lag_seconds` from the feature `second` and from the first-tick cut (`signals.py:521`, `543`); keep the +11s book lookup. One seed, same catalog as LIVE. Watch late-third decision count, buy-300s, worst map. Expected: small, unless the 11s `second` shift is crossing many trees. Kill if late buy-300s moves by <0.2¢.

3. **Objectives, measurement only, then a column if the residual is real.** Prior that dragons/towers/herald/baron are a secondary gap the 77 columns miss: 0.25 as a primary cause, 0.55 that the residual exists. Agent: features-dev. On ~100 late-third maps, compare the 30s mid change after a dragon or tower with the change after a kill that moves gold by a similar amount. Confirm if the objective residual exceeds ~1¢ after gold and deaths are accounted for. The columns already exist on GRID (`docs/experiments/lol-grid-widget.md:43-48`) and are absent from `DOTA_XP_FEATURE_COLUMNS`. Do not revive Dota `buildings-adv-features.md`. A backtest comes after the residual, not before.

4. **Tail of histfix, not another history feature.** Prior that `drop_gap_ticks` made the worst map ~2× worse: 0.40. Prior that it explains LoL vs Dota: 0.10. Agents: tails-opus, hist-dev. One seed with `GRID_HISTORY_POLICY.drop_gap_ticks` false (NaN history, tick kept), same `20261003T014429Z` members. Watch worst map and CVaR5 on the late third against w540lv6 (−$1,056..−$1,107, CVaR about −$457 in the context table). Expected if the drop is the tail: worst map back under −$1,200 and CVaR near −$450, with late buy-300s still ~0.5¢. That last part is the point: fixing the tail does not restore June.

5. **Do not refit the whitelist or the minute grid on the strength of this pass.** Agents: leagues-dev should still report late-third markout inside LCK/LEC/LCS versus the rest, because August’s dataset (915 maps) is much larger than the backtest (451).

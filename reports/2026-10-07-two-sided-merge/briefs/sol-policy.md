# Brief: sol-policy — reconstruct his inventory / merge / taker / capital policy across all 2,110 markets

Source: `$R/data/poly_target/activity_compact.json` (375,961 rows, all games), `taker30.json`, `markets_summary.json`, `user-pnl.json`, `positions.json`. No order books here (CS2/Valorant books are not local); use prices of his own fills, and `https://clob.polymarket.com/prices-history?market=<token_id>&startTs=..&endTs=..&fidelity=1` for a reference mid where you need it (rate-limit yourself; cache under work/). `sol-lol-micro` does LoL with full books; you cover the policy that is visible from his activity alone, on all games, and cross-check the LoL subset against their report when it appears in `$R/reports/`.

Build `$R/work/sol-policy/market_timeline.parquet`: per market, chronological events (BUY fills by token, MERGE, REDEEM, SELL) with running YES qty, NO qty, cost, pairs = min(yes,no), net = yes − no, cash out. Then:

1. **Merge policy.** For each MERGE: pairs available before, pairs merged (usdc_size), lag since pairs first exceeded that amount, time since last merge, game (CS2/LoL/Valorant), map vs series. Find the rule: threshold in shares/$? periodic timer? end of map? Why 50 merges in a 3 h CS2 BO3 but 2 in a 35-min LoL map? What share of pairs is merged mid-market vs left to REDEEM?
2. **Inventory skew.** Distribution of net inventory in shares and $ (net × his last fill price on that token) sampled every fill; peak |net| per market vs market cost; is there a cap? Does the buy rate on the "adding" side drop when |net| is large (compare inter-fill times by side conditioned on sign of net)?
3. **Taker triggers.** For each TAKER fill: net before, side relative to net (reducing/adding), size, price, price change vs his previous fill on either token in the last 60 s, time since his last maker fill. Classify taker fills: (a) flatten after skew, (b) chase after a price jump, (c) other. Shares by game and by $.
4. **Sizing.** Fill size vs price level, vs game, vs map/series, vs time in market; note that fills are partial executions — reconstruct order size where possible by grouping fills with the same transaction_hash / same second / same price.
5. **Price band.** Cost and PnL (cash_net + redeem − cost, approximate) by fill price bucket (0-0.1, 0.1-0.2, ..., 0.9-1.0). Does he quote all the way to 0.02 / 0.98? What happens to pair_avg_cost in markets that ended near 0/1?
6. **Losing markets.** The ~30% with pair_avg_cost > 1: what distinguishes them? (one side ran away and he kept buying it? large taker share? series markets? long duration?) Give 5 worked examples with the event timeline and 5 winning counter-examples.
7. **Capital and concurrency.** Time series (1-min grid) of total unreturned cost across all markets = sum(cost − merge_usdc − redeem_usdc so far). Peak, p95, mean; number of concurrently active markets; turnover = buy volume / mean capital per week. This tells us how much capital his $3M/week needs.
8. **PnL and fees.** Reconcile: sum over markets (merge + redeem + sells − buy cost) + leftover positions (positions.json) vs `user-pnl.json` realized deltas over the same window, vs rebates (MAKER_REBATE / TAKER_REBATE rows: amounts, cadence, implied rate vs his taker fee). Estimate his taker fee per fill: usdc_size − price × size on TAKER rows (check sign and whether maker rows have zero). Report gross edge per $ bought, fees per $, rebates per $, net per $, by game and by map/series.
9. **Week by week.** Volume, PnL, net per $, taker share, number of markets, per ISO week since July; does edge per $ fall as volume rises (capacity)?

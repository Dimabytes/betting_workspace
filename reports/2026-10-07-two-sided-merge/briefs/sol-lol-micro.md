Build one tidy per-fill parquet first (`$R/work/sol-lol-micro/fills.parquet`): his fill (ts, token, outcome index, price, size, role, cost), the book state of BOTH tokens at the fill timestamp (last snapshot at or before ts: best bid/ask/mid for YES and NO, depth at his price, depth at best), game second (interpolate from `lol_clocks` anchors), and his running inventory per token before the fill. Then answer:

1. **Placement.** For maker fills: where was his resting bid relative to the book right before the fill? Distance from best bid (ticks), from mid, from the opposite token's best bid (implied spread = 1 − bid_yes − bid_no when he has fills on both within a short window). Histogram. Was he typically alone at the touch, joining the touch, or one tick behind? Infer from depth at his price vs his fill size.
2. **Two-sidedness in time.** For windows of 30 s / 60 s / 300 s, how often does he have maker fills on both tokens? Estimate the spread he effectively quotes: for each market and each minute, (lowest YES fill price + lowest NO fill price) and the median. Does the quoted spread change with game time, with price level (p near 0.5 vs near 0.1/0.9), with volatility?
3. **Taker fills.** When he takes (role TAKER, confirm with `taker30.json`): what was his net inventory (YES − NO in shares and in $ at mid) just before? Which side did he take — the one that reduces |net|? What had the mid done in the previous 10 / 30 / 60 s? Markout of taker fills at +10 s, +30 s, +60 s, +300 s and at settlement, vs maker fills. Decide: is the taker leg hedging (negative/zero markout, reduces skew) or sniping (positive markout, increases skew)? Quantify both shares.
4. **Inventory policy.** Per market, time series of net inventory in $; distribution of |net| and its peak per market; relation of net to quoted sizes (does he shrink/pull the side that adds to |net|?). Estimate his max-skew parameter in $ and in shares.
5. **Merge policy.** From MERGE activity (type MERGE, usdc_size = pairs merged): lag from "pairs available" (min(yes,no) grows) to merge; merge size thresholds; is merge at the end of the map or periodic; how much is left unmerged into settlement (REDEEM). Why merges are few in LoL (median merge_n 2) vs CS2 (50 in the G2 series).
6. **Time profile.** Fill rate per game minute (maker and taker), maker spread per game minute, last fill time vs map end time (from clocks: when does the clock stop), whether he keeps quoting in the last 5 minutes when the price is near 0/1, and his settlement exposure (REDEEM per market vs cost).
7. **PnL decomposition per market** (LoL): cash from merges + redeems − cost ± leftover positions at last mid; split into maker spread capture (sum over maker fills of (mid_at_fill − price) × size), adverse selection (markout 60 s of maker fills), taker PnL, fees (usdc_size − price×size on taker fills: check that this is the fee). Compare the totals with cash_net in `markets_summary.json` and with his data-api PnL.
8. **Our books vs his fills sanity**: does his maker fill appear in our on-chain fills (`lol_chain.json` / local `onchain_fills`) with him as maker at the same price/size? Report the match rate; mark everything that depends on an unmatched fill as `likely`.
